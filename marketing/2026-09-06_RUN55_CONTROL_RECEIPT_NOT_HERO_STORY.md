# AI SWARMER OS — Run 55

## Customer Truth
Security buyers need a reconstructable control decision more than a cinematic promise.

## Creative Strategy
Campaign: **Control Receipt, Not Hero Story**

Hook: **The strongest agent-security demo ends with evidence of what was allowed, denied, changed, quarantined, and revalidated.**

Primary message: AI SWARMER OS is positioned as an admission, policy, runtime-evidence and enforcement-control layer around agent capabilities. A pilot should begin with one bounded workflow and its authority map, then end with a control receipt that records what the system actually observed and decided.

## CTA
Bring one autonomous workflow, its tools/connectors, sensitive destinations, human-approval points, and trust-expiry conditions. Review the control receipt before discussing scale.

## Production Readiness
The connected Base44 product film was truth-hardened in Run 55. The film is now labeled a **Cinematic Product Concept · Partial Cut**, explicitly says it is not evidence of live network containment, response time, or complete enterprise protection, and frames future containment through connected collectors/enforcement adapters.

The source repository describes the current product as an operational MVP and explicitly states that production deployment still requires collectors/enforcement adapters, enterprise authentication, TLS, secrets management, tenant isolation, full sandbox infrastructure, scanners, and independent security testing.
