# AI SWARMER OS — Growth Run 12 Campaign

## Campaign

**Approved Source ≠ Trusted Capability**

## Product-grounded truth

AI SWARMER OS is designed as a defensive control plane between autonomous intelligence and enterprise authority. Its Capability Gate deliberately separates identity/source trust from payload/capability trust.

An allowed publisher or repository is not enough by itself. Capabilities can be inspected, permission and egress risk evaluated, fingerprinted, sent to Red Room behavioral verification when required, and revalidated when code, version, dependencies, permissions, publisher or fingerprint materially changes. Claimed-vs-observed behavioral mismatch can result in quarantine.

## LinkedIn / enterprise script

Hook: **You approved the source. Did you approve what the capability actually does?**

Agentic systems add a new security boundary. A familiar repository, connector, skill or package can still change what it requests, where it sends data, or how it behaves at runtime.

AI SWARMER OS is built around a Capability Gate: verify source, inspect the payload, score the risk, require behavioral verification when policy demands it, fingerprint the approved version, and revalidate material change.

Identity trust is not payload trust.

CTA: Put a capability gate before autonomous authority.

## Visual direction

Show a trusted-source badge entering the gate, then separate panels for permissions, secrets/exfiltration, risk score, Red Room, behavior comparison, fingerprint, and runtime enforcement. End on a capability passport marked Approved or Quarantined.
