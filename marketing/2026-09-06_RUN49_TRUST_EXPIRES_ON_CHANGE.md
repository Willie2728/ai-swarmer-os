# AI SWARMER OS — Run 49

## Customer Truth
An approval should not survive a material change to the capability it approved.

## Creative Strategy
**Campaign:** Trust Expires on Change
**Hook:** If the tool, permission, publisher, dependency, or fingerprint changes, yesterday's approval is not today's evidence.
**CTA:** Define which material changes must force revalidation before the agent regains authority.

## Production Readiness
This campaign is grounded in the repository's current Capability Gate model: source and payload trust are separated; approved capabilities are fingerprinted; code, version, dependency, permission, publisher, and fingerprint changes can trigger revalidation; claimed-vs-observed mismatch can result in quarantine.

## Distribution Queue
Text/sales-enablement brief only. Not scheduled or published.

## Analytics / Evaluation
Prioritize qualified enterprise conversations where a buyer can name one autonomous workflow, its authority boundary, and the material changes that should expire prior trust.

## Winner Library
Untested. Do not promote until attributable qualified-pilot behavior meets the registered evidence threshold.

## Claims Boundary
AI SWARMER OS is an operational MVP, not a claim of complete network protection or independently validated superiority. Production deployment still requires environment-specific enforcement adapters and hardening. Do not claim certification or complete breach prevention.
