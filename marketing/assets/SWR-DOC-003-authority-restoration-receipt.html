Authority Restoration Receipt

Blocking a risky action is not the end of an agent-security test. A serious control review should define what authority was removed, what evidence justified the decision, and what must be true before that authority can be restored.

1. Authority surface
Which tools, connectors, destinations, data, or consequential actions can the workflow reach?
2. Trigger
What adversarial behavior, permission change, identity change, code/dependency change, or other material event should revoke prior trust?
3. Enforcement action
What should the connected enforcement path do: deny, restrict, sandbox, or escalate?
4. Evidence receipt
Which policy decision, adapter receipt, denied action, containment record, or other artifact proves what actually happened?
5. Revalidation condition
What inspection, identity check, fingerprint match, permission review, or buyer-defined test must pass before authority can be reconsidered?
6. Human owner
Who is accountable for interpreting the receipt and deciding whether consequential authority can be restored?
Qualified next step: scope one workflow where both trust expiration and the evidence required to restore authority are explicit.
