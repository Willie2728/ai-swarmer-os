# AI SWARMER OS — Run 65: Enforcement Boundary

## Customer Truth
An operational security MVP can prove policy logic, evidence handling, admission decisions, revalidation triggers, and incident state without pretending that production enforcement adapters are already deployed across every enterprise boundary.

## Creative Strategy
**Asset:** SWR-TXT-002

**Hook:** Control claims need an enforcement boundary.

**Copy:**
An agent-security dashboard is not enterprise containment by itself.

AI SWARMER OS has an operational MVP with capability admission, Red Room policy state, revalidation triggers, evidence-backed incidents, signed telemetry and deterministic policy primitives. Production protection still depends on the collectors, identity controls, gateways, endpoints, cloud controls, egress enforcement and independent testing connected around that engine.

That is the right pilot question: not “does the dashboard look secure?” but “for this one bounded workflow, what authority can the system observe, deny, quarantine, approve and revalidate — and which adapter actually enforces the decision?”

**CTA:** Bring one autonomous workflow and its authority map. End the pilot with a control receipt.
