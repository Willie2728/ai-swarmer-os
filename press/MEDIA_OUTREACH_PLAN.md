# WCL Media Outreach Plan — Agent Control & Resilience Stack

## Story, not hype
Lead with the timely problem: autonomous AI systems are gaining tools and authority faster than enterprise control infrastructure is maturing. WCL has working early-stage components spanning authority control (SOVRAIL), behavioral policy/containment (SWARMER) and trusted recovery (KAMERON), and is seeking design partners, independent evaluators and strategic capital to harden the stack.

## Press package
1. One-page press release.
2. Five-minute broadcast-quality explainer.
3. 60–90 second cutdown.
4. Product architecture graphic.
5. Founder headshot and WCL/product logos.
6. Screenshots from real working software, clearly distinguishing implemented from roadmap.
7. Short technical evidence sheet: current tests, implemented controls, maturity boundaries.
8. Founder availability and dedicated communications email/phone.

## Outreach order
Tier 1: reporters covering AI safety, agent security, enterprise AI and cybersecurity at Reuters, AP, Bloomberg, WSJ, CNBC, CNN, Fox, Financial Times and major technology publications.
Tier 2: TechCrunch, VentureBeat, The Information, Axios, Wired, Ars Technica and cybersecurity trade publications.
Tier 3: California/Sacramento and Bay Area business/technology press for the founder/studio angle.
Tier 4: press-release wire distribution after direct reporter outreach is prepared.

Founder building agent-control stack as AI industry confronts autonomous-agent containment

## Rules
Do not mass-spam reporters. Personalize each pitch to the reporter’s beat and recent coverage. Do not attach proprietary source code. Do not imply endorsement by OpenAI, Anthropic, NIST or any news organization. Keep public claims tied to implemented features or explicitly label them as development roadmap.
